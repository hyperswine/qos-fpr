# V2 -- FP-RISC and QOS ready for real hardware and real applications

Working notes, not a spec.  Dot points now, systematic later.  The
numbers come from the tree as of v1.1.0; the decisions are proposals
until they are moved into SEMANTICS.md.

## What 2.0 means

- 1.x was the proof of concept: prove each idea works, change anything
  freely.  2.0 is the commitment: the contracts are written down and we
  stop changing them.
- **2.0-alpha** = the contracts exist on paper (SEMANTICS.md, the std
  reference, the bounds register).  Things do not have to work yet.
- **2.0** = the implementations conform.  Every clause in SEMANTICS.md
  points at a test; check-all is the conformance suite.
- **2.0.x** = only conformance fixes.  Nothing language-visible changes
  without a 2.1 and a deprecation cycle.
- The mechanisms for this already exist and just aren't used as a
  contract yet: `fpr commit` + `fpr.lock` version the std modules;
  `QOS_ABI_VERSION` (at 12) and `codegenRev` (at 8) are bumped freely;
  check-all's 103 legs are already an executable spec.  v2 is
  declaring them the contract.

## The workstreams

### 1. SEMANTICS.md -- decide and write down

- The language: evaluation order, types and inference, linearity,
  `unsafe` vs `measure`, equality (deep now -- was header-only until
  last week), integers and overflow, strings (UTF-8? bytes? 1-based
  in Sol), records and update, paths (`@Model.x`), modules and pins,
  holes, the `>` effect view.
- The OS: what a *service* is, what a *driver* is, what a *process*
  is, what a *capability* is, how memory is granted, how IRQs reach
  an actor, what the ABI promises.
- Today there are THREE idioms for reaching a service and that is the
  first decision to make:
    - path strings: `/services/storage` (13 uses), `/services/uart`,
      `/services/display`, ...
    - named devices: `device "blk"`, `device "clint"` + raw `reg32`
    - builtins: `Sys.attachQa`, `Sys.storeReq`, `Sys.timerBind`,
      `Sys.compile`, ~15 of them
  Proposal: one thing -- a capability-typed handle you obtain once,
  with a typed protocol.  The three above become three ways to OBTAIN
  a handle, not three APIs.  `std/uart`'s linear phase-typed handle is
  already the right shape; generalise it.
- Each clause gets a `[test: tests/x.fpr]` slot.  A clause without a
  test is a proposal, not a rule.

### 2. Bounds -- the register

Every compile-time constant, classified.  "Unbounded" honestly means:
every bound that remains is a named, documented contract with a
reason and a test that hits it.

| kind | what | v2 stance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **grows at runtime** (fine) | actor mem rings (`MEM_CAP 1024` initial), the ARC table, Dynamic mailboxes, `RING_MAX 1<<20`, buddy `MAX_ORDER 24` "set by init" | keep; document the growth policy and the ceiling |
| **baked where hardware should decide** (must go) | `FPR_NHARTS 2` at compile time (`QOSP_MAXHARTS 64`), `QOS_ARENA_SIZE`, the fixed `0x4_0000_0000` slot base, `QOS_SLOT_SIZE` 16 MiB per app image, `QOS_PLUG_SIZE` 8 x 4 MiB, `FPR_STACK_SZ` 256 K per actor, `FPR_SLAB_SZ` 256 K / `FPR_MSG_SLAB_SZ` 64 K | harts, memory and disk come from the DTB (virt/board) or the host (qosp) at boot; app + plugin regions sized from the manifest; stack and slab sizes per app, from the manifest, with the machine default as the default |
| **fixed tables** (decide each) | per actor: `MAXSND 8` channel slots, `RING_CAP 64` default; global: `IRQ_MAX 64`, `SCAP 64` steal FIFO, `XCAP 64` wake ring, `MOD_MAXATTACH 8` / `PLUG_MAX 8` plugins, `MAX_GRANTS 64`, `QA_MAX_PERMS 32`, `VMAXCOLS 8` SoA columns | some are REAL-TIME CONTRACTS (the WCET story needs fixed per-actor slots) -- keep those, name them, test them; the rest become grown tables |
| **host tiers** | net: `QOS_NET_MAXCONN 1024` with 8 MB of STATIC rx buffers, `TXCAP 256 K`; virt net: 4-connection TCP table, `VIRTIO_NSLOTS 8`; gfx: `MAX_MESHES 32`, `MAX_TEXT 4096` glyphs/frame, `MAX_INST 16384`, `GFX_EVENT_CAP 64`; evdev `MAXKBD 8`, `INJ_CAP 128` | net buffers from the arena, not static; virtio ring depth from the device's QNUMMAX; gfx tables grown or sized by the scene |
| **accidents** | `VEQ_MAX_DEPTH 64` (equality gives up at depth 64 and says "equal") | cycle detection, not a number; `SSTR_CAP 128` -> check what it bounds |

- Also in the register: things that are limits without a `#define` --
  the 60-line `#:` directive scan in qos.py, arity 64 in codegen
  (`codegenRev 8`), `firstN`-style caps in programs.
- Rule for the register: a bound stays only with (1) a name, (2) the
  reason, (3) what happens when you hit it (refuse with a message,
  never silently wrap), (4) a test that hits it.

### 3. std -- complete, documented, verified

- `std/` has 11 modules.  `programs/mods/` has 37 and THAT is the real
  standard library today: `svc`, `qlog`, `coreutil`, `tui`, `persist`,
  `qsys`, `qar`, `loader`-adjacent, `scene2d`, `glsvc`, `fprlive`,
  `liveview`, `appkit`, `timefmt`...
- Duplicates to resolve: two `timer.fpr`, two `uart.fpr` (std/ and
  mods/).
- Of the 11 in std: no docs mention for `compile`, `lens`,
  `livereload`, `std`, `uart`; no test use for `checkdemo`,
  `livereload`.  102 `unsafe` markers across std against the `measure`
  discipline we already have.
- The job: promote from mods what belongs (the OS API: svc, qlog,
  persist, qsys, qar; the UI: tui, scene2d, glsvc; the net: fprlive,
  liveview), prune the rest into `examples/`, and gate it: a std
  module ships only with a section in `docs/STD.md` and a
  `tests/std_<name>.fpr` -- enforced by a check-all leg that fails on
  a std file lacking either.
- `unsafe` in std goes down to the functions that are genuinely
  unbounded (servers' receive loops), each with a comment saying why.
- Sol's `lib/` gets the same treatment (46 examples, the lib is
  smaller and mostly fine).

### 4. Hardware

- Backends today: `hal/virt` (QEMU virt: UART, PLIC, CLINT, virtio blk
  + net) and `hal/unix` (the qosp host tiers).  The a64 lowering runs
  on Apple silicon.
- Pick ONE board first and give it `hal/<board>/`.  Candidates: a
  RISC-V SBC with upstream OpenSBI + a documented UART/SD/NIC
  (VisionFive 2, Milk-V).  What that needs that virt doesn't:
    - DTB parsing at boot for memory size and hart count -- this is
      what makes "unbounded memory" real, not a bigger constant
    - SD/MMC beside virtio-blk; a real NIC beside virtio-net
    - the OpenSBI boot path; the PLIC/CLINT addresses from the DTB
    - a `qos.py run --on <board>` that flashes/boots (or a disk image
      you write to an SD card)
- macOS as a target (not just a host) is nearly free via qa64mac and
  worth finishing for the "applications" half of the brief.

### 5. Toolchain and install

- brew: install, upgrade, uninstall proven locally on macOS and Linux.
  Uninstall leaves nothing but the user's own `.qos/` dirs.  The
  formula gets a `bottle` block once the tap's CI exists.
- Linux: `fpr` links libEGL/libGL for the Sol GPU tier -> `dlopen` them
  so a sol-only install needs no mesa.
- `qos.py`'s subcommands and flags are part of the contract from 2.0.
- The tag-push 403 from the agent session: releases are cut from a
  human's machine (documented in VERSIONING.md), not something to
  engineer around.

### 6. Discipline (the part that makes it stick)

- ABI: bump once to v13 at 2.0, then frozen.  A change = a new ABI
  version AND a compat note AND a migration.
- A CHANGELOG.md, kept from 2.0-alpha.1.
- Deprecation: nothing removed without one release of warnings.
- The reference docs that don't exist yet and 2.0 needs: SEMANTICS.md
  (language + OS contract), docs/STD.md (the reference), docs/HAL.md
  (the driver contract).  Today's 25 docs are narrative accounts of
  one feature each -- good history, keep them, but they are not a
  reference.
- "Verified" means: every clause -> a test; every bound -> a test that
  hits it; every std module -> a conformance test; check-all green on
  qosp, virt, the board, macOS.

## Order

1. **2.0-alpha.1 -- decisions on paper.**  SEMANTICS.md filled in from
   the skeleton; the bounds register with a stance per row; the std
   inventory with keep/promote/drop per module; CHANGELOG started.
2. **2.0-alpha.2 -- the tree conforms.**  Bounds removed or contracted;
   discovery-time harts/memory/disk; std promoted with the docs+test
   gate; `unsafe` down; ABI v13 declared frozen.
3. **2.0-alpha.3 -- hardware.**  One board booting the native kernel
   from its own disk.  brew install/upgrade/uninstall proven both OSes.
4. **2.0.**  Conformance suite green on qosp, virt, the board, macOS.

## Decide now (everything downstream depends on them)

- The ONE service idiom (workstream 1).  Language-visible.
- Drop the fixed 16 MiB app slot for manifest-sized images.  Changes
  the linker scripts, the loader, and the `.qa` format (QA-FORMAT.md).
- What is a real-time contract vs an accident in the fixed tables.

## Open questions

- Strings: are they bytes or UTF-8 code points?  `Str.len` and
  `Str.at` answer differently today between fpr and sol.
- Integer overflow: wrap, trap, or refuse at compile time where
  derivable?  `measure` already reasons about Int ranges.
- Is `qosp` a 2.0 deliverable (an app host on Linux/macOS) or a dev
  tool?  I'd say deliverable: it's how "applications" ship.
- Does Sol stay a profile of `fpr` in 2.0 or become its own thing?
  (Stay: one grammar, one binary is a strength.)
