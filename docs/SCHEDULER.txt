---- implemented (aux-hart interrupts + the timer service) ----

fpr_irq_hart = the LAST live hart (actors.c): plic.c enables only that
hart's M context and the irq/timer drains are gated on it, so MEIP and
the armed CLINT deadline pop an AUXILIARY hart out of wfi -- the prime
hart (0) takes no device interrupts unless it is the only core.  Bound
actors still run wherever the scheduler puts them (deliveries are
ordinary sends; donation and stealing included).

Timer.qa (mods/timer.fpr, "/services/timer" via System.qa) owns ALL the
CLINT driver work: Sys.timerBind registers its actor, Sys.timerArm arms
ONE deadline (nearest of its sorted pending set), and the irq hart
delivers a bare Int when due.  A client's std.timer.after is ONE send;
the TFire that comes back is an ordinary message wake -- the blocked
caller moves from the backlog into the ready zone under the admission
bounds (RQ_CAP batches, aging tau, g_max_wait), which is exactly the
WCET-bounded "readily executed soon" story.  On hosts with no CLINT the
same actor falls back to sleeper children over Sys.sleepUs.  Single-
hart machines keep the hart-0 deadlock detector's 30 ms pacing: a
sooner timer deadline overrides its mtimecmp arm, a later one rides the
next detector sample.
